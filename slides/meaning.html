02 / The idea
What does “reasoning effort” mean?

The amount of analysis an agent invests before and during implementation.

It is not a quality button. It is a decision about how much thinking the task deserves.
01
ContextHow much of the codebase must the agent understand?
02
AlternativesHow many approaches or causes need comparison?
03
Edge casesWhat can fail outside the happy path?
04
VerificationHow carefully must the result be tested?
More thinking is not automatically better.
